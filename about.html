About AHMAD CRT SNIPER

The founder, the teaching approach and what this education is and is not.

Ahmad is the founder of AHMAD CRT SNIPER and a forex trader with three years of trading experience, focused on CRT-based market analysis, risk management, trading discipline and practical trader education.

Our teaching approach

  • Range first, entry second
  • Clean charts and a clear plan
  • Risk defined before every trade
  • Practice, journaling and review

What this is, and is not

The goal is education, structured decision-making and responsible risk management.

It is not a promise of profit. Trading can result in loss.
