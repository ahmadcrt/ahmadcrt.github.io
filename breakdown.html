Method

How to read a breakdown

Every breakdown follows the same six steps.

1. Range

High, low and 50% midpoint.

2. Sweep

Did price take liquidity beyond one side?

3. Confirmation

Extra evidence, such as a close back inside.

4. Entry rationale

Why this entry area was chosen.

5. Risk plan

Stop loss and how much is risked.

6. Outcome

Stated only when the video documents it.

Watch each video for the reasoning and result it shows. Nothing here is a signal, a promise or a verified track record.
