The basics in short
  • Range: the high and low of one candle are your map.
  • Sweep: price takes liquidity beyond one side, then may reverse. It is a clue, not a guarantee.
  • Confirmation: wait for extra evidence, such as a close back inside the range.
  • Risk: decide your stop loss and target before you enter.
