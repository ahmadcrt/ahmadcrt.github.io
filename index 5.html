About Ahmad

Meet Ahmad, Founder of AHMAD CRT SNIPER

Founder & CEO

Ahmad is a forex trader with three years of trading experience who focuses on CRT-based market analysis, risk management, trading discipline, and practical trader education.

Through structured lessons, practical chart work, and educational market analysis, AHMAD CRT SNIPER aims to help students develop a clearer understanding of the markets and build disciplined trading habits.

The goal is education, structured decision-making, and responsible risk management, not promises of guaranteed profits.

Our teaching approach

  • Range first, entry second
  • Clean charts and a clear plan
  • Risk defined before every trade
  • Practice, journaling and review
  • Building consistent traders
