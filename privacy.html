Privacy Policy

Please read this before you pay for a course.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

AHMAD CRT SNIPER provides forex trading education. This page explains what information we handle when you use ahmadcrt.github.io or contact us.

Information we collect

We do not run accounts or collect payments on this website. If you use the enrolment form, the details you type (name, chosen course, message) are placed into a WhatsApp message that you send yourself. Nothing is stored by this website. If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we receive the details you send, such as your name and phone number or email address.

How we use it

We use the information you send only to reply to you, to confirm enrolment and to deliver the course you ask for.

Third parties

Embedded YouTube videos are provided by YouTube and may set their own cookies. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. WhatsApp, Telegram, TikTok, X and YouTube have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop messaging you, or to delete what you have sent us, by contacting us on WhatsApp or by email.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.

Accounts and saved records

If you create a free account, we collect your name, email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, and are not stored in readable form. Your trading journal entries, lesson progress, quiz scores and simulator results are saved in a database hosted by Supabase and are linked to your account. Database rules are set so that each account can read and change only its own records. Your login session is kept in your browser so you stay signed in. You can export your journal as a CSV file from My Dashboard. To delete your account and saved records, email us and we will remove them. Supabase and GitHub Pages are third-party services with their own privacy policies. Never enter broker passwords or other login details on this website.
